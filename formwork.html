The Sequence

Seven stages. Same order, every time.

No shortcuts between drawing and delivery — every build passes through each stage, tracked and signed off.

01

Survey

Site analysis, soil testing, and feasibility studies before a single line is drawn.

02

Design

Architectural drafting and structural engineering, reviewed against code and the client brief.

03

Permit

Regulatory approval and documentation, handled end-to-end with local authorities.

04

Foundation

Excavation, footing, and foundation pour — the work no one sees but everyone depends on.

05

Structure

Framing, steel, and load-bearing systems rise on a schedule tracked weekly.

06

Finish

Envelope, interiors, and finish carpentry brought to spec and to plan.

07

Handover

Final walkthrough, as-built documentation, and keys delivered on the date we promised.
